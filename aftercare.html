Heal it right.

Your tattoo is an investment — take care of it properly.
Follow these steps for the first 3–4 weeks and your ink will settle beautifully.

01

Keep it wrapped

Leave the protective film on for 2–4 hours after your session. This allows the initial plasma and ink to settle without exposing the fresh wound to bacteria.

When it's time, peel it off gently under lukewarm running water — never rip it off dry.

Tip: If your artist uses second-skin bandage, leave it on for 3–5 days as instructed.
02

Wash gently

Use lukewarm water and a fragrance-free, mild soap (like unscented Castile or baby soap). Wash twice a day — morning and night.

Use your fingertips to gently remove any excess ink, plasma, or debris. Never use a washcloth or sponge — they harbor bacteria and can irritate the skin.

Tip: Pat dry with a clean paper towel — never rub or wipe.
03

Moisturize daily

Apply a thin, even layer of unscented tattoo balm or moisturizer 2–3 times a day for the first two weeks. Less is more — you don't want to suffocate the skin.

After the first two weeks, switch to a fragrance-free lotion to keep the skin hydrated as it continues to heal beneath the surface.

Tip: Use products specifically made for tattoos — avoid petroleum-based ointments like Vaseline.
04

Avoid sun & water

No pools, oceans, lakes, or saunas for at least 3 weeks. The chemicals, salt, and bacteria can cause serious infections and ruin the healing process.

Keep your tattoo out of direct sunlight for the first month. Once healed, always apply SPF 50+ to protect the colors and lines from fading.

Tip: When showering, let water run over the tattoo — don't spray it directly.
05

Don't pick or scratch

It's completely normal for your tattoo to itch, peel, and flake — this is part of the healing process. Do not scratch, pick, or rub the area.

Scratching can pull out pigment, create scars, and leave permanent blank spots in your design. If it itches, pat it gently with a clean hand.

Tip: Keep your nails short and clean to avoid accidental scratching while sleeping.
06

Touch-up policy

I stand behind my work. If your tattoo needs a touch-up due to fading, uneven ink, or minor imperfections, one free touch-up is included within 90 days of your session.

Just send me a photo and we'll schedule a quick session to perfect your piece. After 90 days, a small fee may apply depending on the size and complexity.

Tip: Touch-ups are best done after the tattoo is fully healed — usually 4–6 weeks post-session.
